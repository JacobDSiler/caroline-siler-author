The Author
A Storyteller
at Heart

Caroline Siler lives in a small town in Arkansas in a house in the woods near the Arkansas River valley with her husband. She teaches US History, because she loves the stories of people and events.

She loves to read a good book on rainy days, enjoys baking, hiking, traveling, and gathering around a table to hear and tell family stories. Caroline writes wholesome small town romances with sweet, southern heat.
